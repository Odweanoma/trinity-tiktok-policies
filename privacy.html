Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Introduction

Trinity TikTok Gateway ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we intend to handle information in connection with the Trinity TikTok Gateway integration, which is currently in Sandbox development and not yet approved for production use.

2. Information We May Collect

When the Service becomes operational, we may collect the following types of information:

  • TikTok profile information: Public profile data such as username, display name, bio, and profile picture, if you authorize us to access your TikTok account;
  • Video metadata: Public video details such as titles, descriptions, view counts, like counts, comment counts, and upload dates for public videos you authorize us to review;
  • Usage information: How you interact with the Service, including features accessed and actions taken;
  • OAuth credentials: Access tokens and related authentication information obtained through TikTok's OAuth flow, used solely to authorize API requests on your behalf;
  • Contact information: Your email address if you provide it to us for support or communication.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Service;
  • Analyze public TikTok profiles and videos as you request;
  • Prepare content drafts based on public data;
  • Communicate with you about the Service, including updates and support;
  • Improve and develop the Service.

4. Data Storage

Any data collected through the Service would be stored on secure servers controlled by the Service operator. Because the integration is in Sandbox development, no production data storage system is currently operational.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only:

  • With TikTok, as required by the OAuth authorization flow and API terms;
  • With service providers who assist in operating the Service, under confidentiality obligations;
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of users.

6. Data Retention

We retain collected information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. Because the Service is in development, no fixed retention schedule is currently in place.

7. Revocation and Deletion

You may revoke TikTok authorization at any time through your TikTok account settings. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us at odweanoma@gmail.com. We will respond to deletion requests as soon as reasonably practicable.

8. Planned vs. Existing Functionality

The data handling practices described in this Policy reflect planned functionality for the Trinity TikTok Gateway integration. Because the integration is in Sandbox development, these practices may not yet be operational. We make no claims about encryption standards, retention periods, operational security measures, or regulatory compliance at this time.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated Policy on this website.
